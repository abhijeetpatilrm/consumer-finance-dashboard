==================================================

==================================================

- users
- transactions
- rewards
- redemptions

- original `transaction_id` stored separately
- NUMERIC/Decimal for money
- TIMESTAMPTZ for normalized timestamps
- appropriate indexes

Do NOT assume source transaction IDs are unique.
